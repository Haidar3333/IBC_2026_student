7487-The view generally entertained by naturalists is that species, when
7488-intercrossed, have been specially endowed with the quality of
7489:sterility, in order to prevent the confusion of all organic forms. This
7490-view certainly seems at first probable, for species within the same
7491-country could hardly have kept distinct had they been capable of
7492-crossing freely. The importance of the fact that hybrids are very
7493-generally sterile, has, I think, been much underrated by some late

7495:important, inasmuch as the sterility of hybrids could not possibly be
