## Exercise 6.2: Codon to Amino Acid

For this exercise, I created a Python script called `codon_to_amino.py`.

First, I created an empty dictionary and opened `CodonTable.tsv`. I used the codon as the dictionary key and the amino acid symbol as the value.

Next, I defined the DNA sequence:

`CTA GGA GTG ATT TCG`

I used `.split()` to separate the sequence into individual codons. Then I used a `for` loop to look up each codon in the dictionary and added the amino acid symbol to a list.

### Output

DNA sequence: CTA GGA GTG ATT TCG

Codons: ['CTA', 'GGA', 'GTG', 'ATT', 'TCG']

Amino acid sequence: ['L', 'G', 'V', 'I', 'S']
