Problem 1: Baking a Cake

Pseudocode

1. Gather flour, eggs, milk, butter, and sugar.
2. Put all ingredients into a mixing bowl.
3. Mix the ingredients until they form a batter.
4. Pour the batter into an oven pan.
5. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
6. Put the pan into the oven.
7. Bake the cake for 20 minutes.
8. Insert a knife into the cake to check if it is done.
9. If the knife comes out clean:
   - Remove the cake from the oven.
10. Else:
   - Bake the cake for a few more minutes.
   - Check it again with the knife.
11. Repeat until the knife comes out clean.
12. Remove the finished cake from the oven.
